Almost all apps I've used have felt immensely slow nowadays. Developers are pushing more features and more updates, and for what? 

It almost feels like the ease of implementing new features results in a kind of "addiction" to developing new ones. Want to add something to the app? Just one-shot it with Claude Code or Codex!

It's exactly like those incremental games where your objectives keep growing and growing, and suddenly you have a multitude of things to control and grow. It takes you out of the game, you went from clicking a cookie to a whole tree of upgrades and ascension.

I miss the old days when moving a button 2px took weeks or months of A/B testing and user trials.
